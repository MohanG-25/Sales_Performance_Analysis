Project Title: Sales Performance Analysis using SQL & Excel

Project Objective: To analyze sales data and identify trends, top-performing products, underperforming regions, and profit-impacting factors to support business decision-making.

Tools:
    1.SQL (MySQL / PostgreSQL / SQLite)
    2.Excel:
        -> Pivot Tables
        -> Charts
        -> Basic formulas

Insights:
        -> 📈 Furniture products recorded higher sales compared to other product categories, which indicates that the superstore generated higher profit from furniture-type products.
        -> 🌍🏆 The East region outperformed other regions in both sales and profit, making it the best-performing region overall.
        -> 🥇🛒 The Bretford CR4500 Series Slim Rectangular Table ranks first among the top 10 products, with a sales value of 957.5775, indicating that it is a highly demanded product.
        -> 📅📊 Monthly sales trend analysis shows fluctuations from March to July, with sales dynamically rising and falling during this period. 
           This suggests that launching or promoting products between March and July could increase the chances of higher profit📅📊.

Recommendations:
        -> Reduce excessive discounting on loss-making products.
        -> Focus marketing on high-profit categories.
        -> Improve performance in low-performing regions.
